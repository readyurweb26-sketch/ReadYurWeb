Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This page explains, in plain terms, what information ReadYurWeb collects through this website, why, and where it goes. It covers every form and script currently live on this site — nothing more, nothing hidden.

Who this policy covers

ReadYurWeb ("we", "us") designs and builds custom websites — delivered as a finished, professional product, not a work-in-progress hobby project. We work mainly with small and growing businesses (gyms, restaurants, clinics, coaching institutes and similar), and we keep costs low for them by design: instead of a paid backend server or database, submissions from this site are processed through Google Apps Script and stored in a private Google Sheet, and the site itself is hosted on free-tier platforms such as Vercel (with similar options like Shopify available depending on the project). This keeps the ongoing cost of running a client's website close to zero, without cutting corners on how their data — or a visitor's data — is handled. This policy applies to readyurweb.vercel.app and covers the contact form, the pricing/payment modal, the WhatsApp quick-detail form, and the site's background scripts (analytics event, offline caching, fonts).

ReadYurWeb operates as a professional freelancing brand and is not a registered corporate company. We are committed to delivering enterprise-level web design, development, and digital marketing services with the personalized care and agility of a dedicated freelance expert.

What we collect, and why

We only collect what you type into one of our forms, or what your browser needs to send to make the site work. We do not run visitor tracking, cookies-based advertising, or sell any data.

1. Contact form

Fields: name, business type, WhatsApp number, message.

Used to reply to your enquiry and understand what kind of site you need. Stored as a row in a private Google Sheet ("Inquiries") that only we can access.

2. Pricing / payment modal

Fields: name, phone, email, selected plan, payment option, a screenshot of your UPI payment, and the transaction ID / UTR number.

Used to confirm your order and match your payment to it. The screenshot is uploaded directly to a private Google Drive folder that only we can access — it is never made public or shared with any third party. Your details and the screenshot's link are stored as a row in a private Google Sheet ("Pricing Leads"), and an Order ID is generated so you can reference your submission.

3. WhatsApp quick-detail form

Fields: name, email, phone, business name, business category, website requirement, budget, preferred contact time, custom message, and (optionally) your current website.

Used to pre-fill a WhatsApp message to us and saved as a row in a private Google Sheet ("WhatsApp Inquiries") so we have a record even if the WhatsApp chat gets lost.

4. Automatic / background data

  • WhatsApp click events — when you tap a WhatsApp button, we log that the button was clicked (and which one) to a marketing data layer (Google Tag Manager / Analytics style event). No personal details are attached to this event by itself.
  • Offline caching (service worker) — your browser may store a local copy of this site's pages, fonts and images so it loads faster and works offline. This lives only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Standard server logs — like any website, Vercel (our hosting provider) automatically logs basic request data (IP address, browser type, timestamp) for security and performance. We do not access this for tracking purposes.

How your data is protected

  • Every submission passes through server-side checks before it's stored — origin verification, a bot-friction token, rate limiting, and input sanitisation.
  • Payment screenshots and all Sheets are private by default — never shared publicly or indexed.
  • Screenshot uploads are capped at 5 MB and validated before being saved.
  • We don't build or store passwords — there's no login system anywhere on this site, so there's no account to be breached.
Honest limitation: this site runs on Google Apps Script rather than a dedicated server, which means a security token can reduce automated abuse but can't cryptographically prove a request came from this exact page rather than a copy of it. We treat every backend action as something a determined attacker could reach directly, and we design and monitor accordingly — nothing here is claimed as "unhackable."

How long we keep it

Form submissions stay in our private Sheets and Drive for as long as we may reasonably need them for business or accounting purposes (e.g. order records). If you'd like your data deleted sooner, just ask — see contact details below.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • You can decline to fill in any form — nothing on this site requires you to submit information just to browse it.
  • You can clear this site's offline cache at any time from your browser's site settings.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business owners and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
